# SOP-102: Prior Authorization Missing (CARC 197)

Applies when payment is denied because precertification or authorization was absent (CARC 197), including letters that say no authorization number was on file.

Action: request_retro_auth
1. Route to the Authorization team work queue with the claim number and date of service.
2. The team checks whether an authorization exists in the EHR; if not, they request a retro-authorization from the payer.
3. Do not resubmit until an authorization number is obtained.
