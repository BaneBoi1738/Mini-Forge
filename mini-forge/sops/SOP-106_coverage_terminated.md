# SOP-106: Coverage Terminated (CARC 27)

Applies when expenses were incurred after the member's coverage ended (CARC 27), or the member was not eligible on the date of service.

Action: verify_eligibility
1. Route to the Eligibility team work queue with the claim number and date of service.
2. The team checks for other active coverage and bills the correct payer, or moves the balance to patient responsibility per policy.
