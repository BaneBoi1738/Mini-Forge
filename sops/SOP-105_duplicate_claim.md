# SOP-105: Duplicate Claim (CARC 18)

Applies when the payer denies a claim as an exact duplicate of one already received (CARC 18).

Action: close_no_action
1. Route to the work queue as close_no_action with the claim number so the team can confirm the original claim was paid.
2. Do not resubmit.
