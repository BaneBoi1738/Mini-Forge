# SOP-101: Missing or Invalid Claim Information (CARC 16)

Applies when the payer denies a claim because information is missing or invalid (CARC 16), e.g. invalid modifier, missing referring provider, wrong place of service.

Action: correct_and_resubmit
1. Identify the missing or invalid element from the denial text.
2. Route to the Billing Corrections work queue with the claim number and the element to fix.
3. Do not appeal. A corrected claim is the fastest path.
