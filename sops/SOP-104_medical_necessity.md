# SOP-104: Medical Necessity (CARC 50)

Applies when services are denied as not medically necessary (CARC 50), including letters saying documentation does not support the need for services.

Action: appeal_with_records
1. Draft an appeal letter requesting reconsideration and listing the records to attach (clinical notes, orders, test results).
2. Route the draft to the Appeals work queue.
3. Claims with billed amount above $5,000 must not be drafted automatically. Flag them for human review by an appeals specialist.
