# SOP-103: Timely Filing (CARC 29)

Applies when the payer states the time limit for filing has expired (CARC 29).

Action: timely_filing_review
1. Route to the Timely Filing review queue with the claim number, date of service and the payer's stated receipt date.
2. The reviewer looks for proof of timely submission (clearinghouse acceptance report). With proof, appeal; without proof, send to write-off review.
3. Automated systems must not write off a claim.
